Mined 2026-07-28 from the full campaign record (FINDINGS.md P1–P365), via a 291-item extraction pool, 5 analysis lenses, every law adversarially verified against the pool (24 laws survived; 0 refuted outright; all carry verifier qualifiers, merged into the statements below — the printed law IS the corrected law). ## Lens: cost-model

### Law 1 — Touched-Lines × Residency-Tier Law (master memory-cost law) — VERIFIED-WITH-QUALIFIERS
**Statement.** Wall-clock of any memory stage ≈ F + Σ_rows lines(row)×c(tier); lines = ceil(rowbytes/64); c spans ~3 orders (L2/L3 ~1ns/line; DRAM streamed ≈ prefetch-hidden; DRAM scattered 20–100ns/line; page-cache thrash 1–10us/row). **P366 amendment — scattered decomposition:** on SCATTERED stages the per-row cost is α·(first-touch) + β·(lines−1) with α ≫ β, so per-row byte cuts pay only by reducing ROW COUNT or crossing to ≤1 line; the old_lines/new_lines ratio applies to CONTIGUOUS (bandwidth-bound) stages only (measured: LOWRANK-NAV halved wiki beam bytes 8→4 lines for 0.97–1.00× QPS across four paired configs; retrodicts the t2i 4-line exception below and DEEP's 1-line walk economics). Byte cuts otherwise pay ≈ old_lines/new_lines when stream-bound, super-linearly when crossing a residency tier, and ~nothing for rows within the prefetch window (**~≤4 lines at low memory pressure — load-dependent gray zone for 4–12-line rows**). The two-number auto-set rule (lines≥4 + memory-bound ⇒ SQ4) is **co-gated by the sqrt(m) precision-margin law**: cohere-10M has 12-line rows yet SQ4 is non-Pareto (−0.003 recall tax, P345). "Compute is free" holds **only per-stage after the ISA-width litmus (P183)** — the compute-bound exception class contains several of the campaign's biggest wins (P84-86, P113, P116-117, P196). The DEEP d=96 floor is the prefetch-committed row stream, not fixed F (P340 MICRO-DECOMP).
**Evidence.** SQ4 tracks line ratio at ≥8 lines: wiki d=1024 442→235us ≈ 16→8 (P340-sq4); tier-crossing super-linear: wiki f16 143→72GB, 9397→106-175us/q = 50-90x (P341); scattered c(tier) measured 31-52ns/line (P285-286); memory-side nulls: VNNI micro 1.8x → pipeline wash (N17, P102-103); layout = line reduction: +21.9% from halved pages/row (P348), 3.3x contiguous IVF (P18).
**Exceptions/boundaries.** Strongest counterexample: t2i d=200 (P345) — 4-line rows, SQ4 halves lines, pays ~0 unpressured, +36-44% only under pressure (residency-tier effect, not line-ratio). Compute-bound scans exist (P115, P21); tier assignment from footprint alone unreliable at boundaries (WEBVID-BIMODAL, P344).
**Untested predictions.** (1) perf-counter test: LLC-miss-lines×64B predicts rescore time within ~20% on wiki/WebVid/cohere, near-zero correlation on DEEP. (2) cohere d=768: rank-exact SQ4 rung + f32 correction band gives ~1.8-2x rescore gain and flips P345's non-Pareto verdict. (3) SQ2 rung at d=1024: further ~2x stage gain (+15-25% e2e in 0.93-0.97 band) iff Law-4 containment pilot passes. (4) cgroup pressure sweep: t2i SQ4 gain moves 0→~40% with pressure; DEEP stays ~0.
**Simplifications.** Auto-set SQ4/F16/RESIDENT from lines/row + footprint/RAM (with precision co-gate); standing kill-rule: no SIMD/FLOP/work-skip proposal on a stage whose ISA litmus shows <15%; one predicted-vs-measured table keyed by lines/row+tier replaces six per-dataset narratives.

### Law 2 — Scatter-vs-Stream Crossover Law — VERIFIED-WITH-QUALIFIERS
**Statement.** Scatter penalty ρ(rowbytes) = c_scatter/c_stream ≈ 1 for rows ≤2 lines, growing ~linearly to ~10x at 512B, plus a page-granular clause (rows spread over more 4KB pages pay TLB cost until physically reordered). Adaptivity wins iff its work-reduction ratio E_s/E_a > ρ. **This is a per-dataset, per-band pilot inequality, not a dimension threshold**: E_s/E_a varies ≥10x across datasets with routing effectiveness, OOD-ness and graph quality (<~1.5 on msturing; >10 on wiki mid-band). A cascade can win the loose band at ρ≈1 (msturing 1.45-1.78x) and a walk can win a mid band at ρ≈10 (Roar on wiki ≤1.4x at 0.93-0.955). Measure both E_s/E_a (per band, best available graph) and ρ; scope "walk wins" to walks whose graph quality reaches the target recall (SYMPACK-B precondition); do NOT auto-scope code paths by row bytes.
**Evidence.** d=96 walk beats own cascade and Roar 1.16-1.48x (P353); best graph-free alternative 0.56x (P354); identical walk at d=1024 loses to its OWN cascade needing 1445→3693 evals for +0.05 (P361); scatter→sequential worth 1.5x at d=200 (P202); page clause +21.9% (P348).
**Exceptions/boundaries.** Counterexamples that killed the fixed d≈200-256 crossover: msturing d=100 cascade wins the ENTIRE frontier vs bug-fixed Roar (P339/P341); wiki d=1024 Roar's walk wins 0.93-0.955 (P360/P361); WebVid crossover moved with seed coverage, not bytes (P337). Compute-bound scans break the model (P115); extreme-loose 0.80 corner favors adaptive best-first ~1% (P356).
**Untested predictions.** (1) msturing d=100 portal walk vs own cascade at loose band (likely falsified by Roar data — treat as contradicted-until-measured). (2) t2i d=200 walk-vs-cascade within ±1.1x at loose recall. (3) wiki walk guided by a 64B one-line SQ4-prefix sketch recovers most of the d=1024 walk deficit if ρ is the whole story — decisive mechanism test. (4) crossover shifts with hugepages and graph-local physical order at fixed d.
**Simplifications.** One dimensionless pilot ρ×E_a/E_s (~1 min from trace replay) picks walk vs cascade per band; delete cell-major batching / cohort-GEMM branches below d≈200; paper: one E_s/E_a-vs-ρ plot replaces separate walk-win/walk-loss stories.

### Law 3 — Materialized-Coverage Currency Law — VERIFIED-WITH-QUALIFIERS
**Statement.** Recall at fixed work is bounded by materialized coverage (bytes written at build: probed cells, SOAR copies, edges, seeds). No query-time repair **beyond global query-independent calibration** recovers lost coverage — gamma routing (P206/P209/P319) succeeds massively (matched recall at half the probes; WebVid coverage@p32 0.16→0.36) and SATURATES its class; everything richer fails (gbias −0.8pt, Wq −40pt, PRF −2.7pt, supervised reranker < raw top-15). Channels saturate geometrically (miss(R)=m_inf+A·δ^(R−1), δ≈0.44-0.72, R²≈0.99); channels add only when covering different miss mass (graph is cleanly ADDITIVE over gamma after union-trim, P214/P216). Buy coverage in the channel with best marginal coverage-per-row-touched; "coarser cells once a graph carries coverage" is a **scale-gated knee** (wins 1M/10M, wash at 100M, P240). Precision can bind below the coverage bound in more regimes than m≥384 (i8 LUT at small m P84-86; dpb coarsening P174/P177-178; int8-GT ceiling P153-157).
**Evidence.** Coverage as hard bound: P127 (recall == pool ceiling to 4 decimals), P300 (oracle routing +0.0pt with graph on), P311. Unlearnability beyond calibration: P312-316, P351. Saturation fits: P253/P255 (δ=0.436, R²=0.992), P362. Copies: N23, P319. Build outside the capped router: P296-299.
**Exceptions/boundaries.** In-dist high-coverage regimes: extra materialization is dead weight (P303, P335-kills). Query-aware channels density-bound (WIKI-QSEED-STARVED, 1.4% density → coverage 0.034-0.065).
**Untested predictions.** (1) Matched-byte A/B: +k edges (4B) vs a0 copies (rowbytes) at cohere-10M — edges predicted 10-100x better coverage-per-byte at d=768. (2) Subsample WebVid queries to wiki's 1.4% density: QSEED/co-edge gains collapse to null. (3) 30-second coverage pilot (routed coverage@p, seed coverage@S, 1-hop δ) predicts winning channel mix pre-build. (4) wiki with ~5M genuine held-out queries: co-retrieval edges activate and claw back part of the 0.93-0.97 band.
**Simplifications.** Close the learned-query-time-routing class (EXEMPT metric/score calibration); replace independent k/a0/QSEED/p sweeps with one pilot-driven coverage-budget allocator; paper: one coverage-currency section with one saturation equation.

### Law 4 — sqrt(m) Precision-Margin Law (selection vs ordering) — VERIFIED-WITH-QUALIFIERS
**Statement.** Score noise σ ≈ σ0·√m; ranking collapses when σ exceeds ~half the competing margin (measured: σ/margin=0.54 at m=384 int8-sat → ordering 0.07 vs int16 0.87; collapse boundary is joint σ0(dpb)·√m, not m alone). **Feed-forward cut-then-rescore stages** need only SELECTION at the cut; adaptive consumers (walks, beams, seeding) consume ORDERING and widening does not substitute (SYMPACK-B, GATE2, P256). Near-cut misranking is recoverable by widening (W≈2k captures 91-98% of the gain) **only while the rescore tier stays a minor cost share** — there is a middle band where recovery is proportional-cost and net-negative (P170-175: reorder 93% of time). The unrecoverable floor is a metric/distribution-dependent **containment** quantity, operationalized by the GATE1 offline containment pilot, not an absolute code parameter. Accumulator precision asymmetric to code bits: <~12-bit accumulation is fatal (P112).
**Evidence.** Direct measurement P310-7/P239 (0.070→0.8716 same-index flip); resolution sets rerank depth (P84-86, P111); GATE1 4-bit containment 0.9984 vs RBQ 1-bit ceiling 0.44-0.78; W=2k at DEEP 1M/10M (P362); containment pilot beats dimension bands across five datasets (P363); fp16+12-row f32 band exact recall at +14.5-35.4% QPS (P350).
**Exceptions/boundaries.** Strongest counterexample (scopes "never ordering"): SQ4 passes selection-sufficiency yet the SQ4-guided walk loses to int8-guided on the SAME graph and doubling evals doesn't recover (SYMPACK-B) — walk guidance needs 4-8+ bits. Wide-margin regimes tolerate low precision (P239 history); homogeneous unions defeat any coarse tier (P337-rbq); refiners converge to their own metric's fixpoint (P303).
**Untested predictions.** (1) Predict collapse m* from per-dataset margin distributions; test m=192 (cohere dpb=4). (2) CASCADE_K optimum scales ≈ 2k·(σ_stage/margin) — fit the regression on two datasets. (3) wiki fp16 correction-band width predicted ≤16 rows from offline near-tie density (5-min measurement, P350 transfer). (4) 2-bit rung passes GATE1 containment ≥0.995 on wiki pools but fails DEEP unions — run the sim before any build.
**Simplifications.** One containment-pilot sets scan precision, CASCADE_K, W, correction band, rung eligibility (deletes dimension-banded constants); standing gate: no sub-4-bit tier without offline containment ≥0.995 on real unions; paper: one σ0√m-vs-margin figure replaces five anecdotes.

### Law 5 — Cascade Fixed-Floor Law — VERIFIED-WITH-QUALIFIERS
**Statement.** Cascades carry recall-insensitive per-query work: bookkeeping tolls (tens of us) PLUS fixed coverage/scan work (which dominates). This sets the loose-recall boundary **only after per-band retuning and geometry re-optimization have squeezed reducible effort** (P349 cut a gap 1.84x→1.29x; P325 closed t2i-1M to parity inside the cascade — the residual truly-structural pure-graph loose edge is confined to low-d (d<~100), not "1M scale"). Amdahl clause stands: kernel speedups convert to e2e wins in proportion to phase share (1.8x at 6% = 1.07x, P187). Optimal cascades equalize MARGINAL cost across stages (P211, P362 H=E+(R−1)B). The floor is partly load-bearing (TINY-SCAN, P229) — floor surgery must A/B end-to-end. New-stage pre-kill must charge F **net of per-row downstream savings** (as-written it would have killed P194's winning tier and SQ4-RUNG).
**Evidence.** Tolls real when unmanaged: env lookups 31% of query (P241), stale rerank floor 2x (P111), per-query Python 7x loss (P43-47), heap bookkeeping +6.1-17.4% (P356). Fixed overhead cancels op savings (P147-149, P200, P115). Marginal-cost equalization: 160-trial Optuna returns to reference (P211).
**Exceptions/boundaries.** Strongest counterexample: P325 closed the "irreducible 1.24x" t2i-1M loose edge to parity ONE DAY after P324 declared it, via coarse-cell geometry — most measured loose gaps were reducible. At high recall the floor is noise (P146); scale-gating flips structures floor-add↔floor-save (P31, P109, P230-234).
**Untested predictions.** (1) Direct floor isolation: run cascade with stages forced empty; predict ΣF ≈ 25-60us/q for the 1M champion. (2) Band-switch at t2i d=200 (superseded in outcome by P325 but still locates Law 2's crossover). (3) Arena-allocated query context (pre-built LUT slabs, reused dedup tables): +5-15% at loose bands, ~0% at high recall.
**Simplifications.** Report ΣF as a first-class preset number; one "fixed floor vs adaptive walk" paragraph replaces ad-hoc Roar-loose-band explanations; pre-kill rule (F charged net of savings) applied at sketch stage.

## Lens: recall-model

### Law 1 — Upstream Containment Bound — VERIFIED-WITH-QUALIFIERS
**Statement.** Recall is destroyed only by dropping a true neighbor at a cut (containment loss), never by pure mis-ordering absent a truncation boundary or final-tier fidelity failure. End-to-end recall@k is the **product of per-stage containments × final-tier scoring fidelity**; it equals the most-upstream binder's containment only when all downstream cuts are ~lossless, and the binder is **operating-band-specific**. Downstream WIDTH levers (CASCADE_K, t_surv, W) retain headroom equal to their own stage's containment loss even while an upstream stage dominates (WebVid: CASCADE_K=64 moved the ceiling +2.7pt downstream of the declared seed binder). Diagnostic: probe GT∩candidates at each stage boundary; achieved recall == a stage ceiling to 3-4 decimals names the binder (P127). Corollary restricted: budget-knob invariance is a bug signature **only in an unsaturated regime where an independent baseline says the knob must matter**; recall FALLING as budget rises remains an unconditional bug signature (P132, P30). Final-tier exception covers metric mismatch (P93/P97) as well as precision (P153-157).
**Evidence.** P127 (0.9307 == 0.9308 pool ceiling); P174 (coverage 14.6% but recall 0.09 — lost containment unrecoverable); P215 (1M-tuned t_surv caps 10M recall at ANY p); P168-169 (t binds, K inert); P41 (containment 1.000 passes losslessly); P312 (all 10 NN in median 3-4 cells).
**Exceptions/boundaries.** Referee artifacts masquerade as ceilings (P271-272, P330, P339); containment-neutral changes can still change SPEED via second-order dynamics (TINY-SCAN: recall-identical but 1.7x slower — the scanned pool seeds the beam).
**Untested predictions.** (1) One instrumented pass (200 queries, GT∩candidates at route/seed, post-scan, post-graph, post-int8) predicts final recall within ~0.01 on all six datasets, zero sweeps. (2) Knob fungibility on the equal-containment contour: cohere-10M (p,t) grid collapses to one curve. (3) DEEP 0.84-0.92 band: only entry-stage materialization moves recall at matched work. (4) On any new dataset, the containment probe names the binder BEFORE tuning and beats lever-choice-by-analogy.
**Simplifications.** Replace the recall-banded config ladder and per-dataset t_surv/W/CASCADE_K tuning with one containment pilot solving every stage width; trigger CASCADE_K escalation on measured containment, not d; paper: one stage-containment section replaces six per-dataset bottleneck stories.

### Law 2 — sqrt(m)/Margin Precision Threshold — VERIFIED-WITH-QUALIFIERS
**Statement.** A stage's precision is sufficient iff σ0·√m stays below the score margin at its cut line; margins are a joint property of **metric × dataset × stage and must be sampled** (no m-only, d-only, or in-dist/OOD rule is safe — the same coarse code was tolerable on in-dist L2 msturing, fatal on OOD/IP t2i, P177). Above threshold extra bits buy nothing (dpb=1==dpb=2). Slightly below, misranking is recoverable by widening W ∝ noise/margin — **only ~free while the exact tier is a small time fraction**; there is a fourth regime (rank-recoverable but economically losing, P170-175) between the free near-side and containment collapse. Far below, containment fails and no downstream stage recovers (1-bit ceiling 0.44-0.78; dpb=50 → 0.09). Loss-function/rotation levers can shift the reorder-depth curve materially (+0.05-0.11, P185 faithful anisotropic-VQ) **but cannot change its bit-rate-bound shape — they cannot substitute for bits**. Corollary strengthened: cut bits to the threshold, then stop.
**Evidence.** P310-7/P239 (54% of margin → ordering 0.07 vs 0.87); P84-86 (resolution sets rerank depth); P112 (code-vs-accumulator asymmetry in one experiment); P166-174 (recoverable→fatal transition); P350 (12-row f32 band, +21-35% QPS); P153-157 (top tier must exceed GT margins).
**Exceptions/boundaries.** Strongest counterexample (kills the "<0.01 rotation" clause): P185 faithful anisotropic-VQ +0.08-0.11 at matched bytes — the old <0.01 figure was a block-diagonal implementation artifact. P200 (estimator gain forcing slower numeric regime loses e2e); P303 (build-time objectives have thresholds too); cached verdicts expire when the query distribution changes (P239 history).
**Untested predictions.** (1) Sample margin spectra once; b*(dataset,m) predicts the minimal safe rung — specifically SQ3 on wiki stays rank-recoverable at ~2x W while SQ2 loses containment (the 2/3-bit bracket was never run). (2) W* ≈ k + c·(σ√m/margin)·T reproduces tuned CASCADE_K values within ~20%. (3) Margin-measured dispatch auto-selects int8-sat vs int16/SQ4 per dataset with no flags, matching all history.
**Simplifications.** Replace FS2_CAP + the m≳128 int8/int16 special case + SQ4/f16 opt-in flags with one startup computation (σ√m vs sampled margin); permanently close "rotation/codebook as rank-preservation rescue" and "sub-4-bit cull tier" classes; fp16+correction-band as unconditional final tier; drop "more precision" from tuning search space (sweep widths only).

### Law 3 — Materialized-Coverage Law (coverage stored offline; calibration the only cell-level exception) — VERIFIED-WITH-QUALIFIERS
**Statement.** WHICH cells hold a query's neighbors is not economically predictable at query time from **cell-summary (routing-visible) features** beyond one query-independent calibration of systematic score bias (gamma; class includes P97's cosine-routing swap) — measured residual x1.17-1.21 (P205) is thin and subsumed by gamma's x1.75. Coverage crossing that gap must be materialized point-level ids (SOAR copies, kNN+co-retrieval edges, QSEED, tree-EM). **Explicitly outside the prohibition**: query-time inference over POINT-LEVEL evidence acquired during search — expansion ordering of materialized edges (P256: +0.3-1.0pt strict Pareto at matched budget), certificate-gated deepening where difficulty varies (P9: 1.9x), walk policies (P353/P356), state-driven streaming budgets — these raise ACHIEVED coverage from the same stored ids and pay when they steer already-budgeted work rather than triggering re-routing double-work (P313's tie). Restated: materialized ids set the coverage CEILING; query-time point-level steering sets how much is realized per unit cost; cell-summary inference moves neither.
**Evidence.** P312 (oracle coverage@16=1.0 vs 0.83-0.90 achieved; headroom real but unlearnable); P313-316, P351 (every richer-than-gamma attempt fails); P300 (perfect cell selection +0.0pt with graph on); P256 (expansion order strict Pareto win, all 12 cells); P292-294 vs P296-299 (build-time coverage must also come from outside the router).
**Exceptions/boundaries.** Query-derived materialization density-bound (WIKI-QSEED-STARVED); gamma is OOD-scoped, not a safe in-dist default; in-dist, query-aligned adjacency ≈ plain kNN (P326).
**Untested predictions.** (1) Equal-byte fungibility: a0 copies vs graph edges at matched bytes — equal in-dist, edges win under shift. (2) Class-closing run: a rich-feature router head still loses to raw+gamma held-out — retires the class permanently. (3) 2-3-parameter monotone calibration matches 1-parameter gamma within noise (class saturated). (4) QSEED viability predictable from query density alone (~10% flips loose band, <2% null) — bracket with subsampled query sets.
**Simplifications.** Retire adaptive-p/gbias/learned-reranker/PRF code paths to diagnostics; gate future ideas in the class on held-out predictive power BEFORE hot-path work (P351 gate); unify a0/KEDGE/co-edges/QSEED-S under one coverage-bytes budget with shift-dependent split.

### Law 4 — Geometric Reachability Law — VERIFIED-WITH-QUALIFIERS
**Statement.** In the IVF-seeded, fixed-round, per-cohort repair mode on base-manifold kNN graphs, in-distribution: miss(R) = m_inf(entry) + A·δ^R (fits R²=0.99). Scope conditions: (1) the entry-set ceiling is a property of **(entry set × edge alignment × traversal policy)** — with query-aligned hybrid edges, tiny-seed deep-hop walks gain +12.1pt and flip bands (P323 SUPERSEDED P311); in walk mode entry quality sets COST (~25-30% of evals), not a recall ceiling. (2) "Wide+shallow beats deep" is a per-cohort-expansion artifact — best-first ordering reverses it (P256) and deep narrow fronts win in walk mode (P356). (3) δ is a **(dataset, scale, graph-construction, expansion-policy)** property, not a dataset constant. (4) Edge quality saturates fast **only under uncorrelated corruption of in-dist kNN edges** (P295); router-correlated degradation does not saturate (P281-283/P292-294), and on OOD edge ALIGNMENT at fixed count is first-order currency (P321, P343), itself density-bounded. (5) Edge count is the reach currency (k16→8 collapses 3.4x→1.8x); benefit ~ (1 − entry containment) as a TREND (d100>d200>d768), but t2i's actual entry containment is 0.83, and graph levers were repeatedly decisive there (P207, P220, P321-323). Vamana diversification pays only for cold-start walks (P336 vs GATE2). Optimal k grows with n (KEDGE-bracket, P360).
**Evidence.** P253/P255/P362 (geometric fits to noise floor, H=E+(R−1)B exact); P317-318 (edge count clean); P336 (navigability decomposed: count+pool+CASCADE_K); P295 (quality saturation); P300 (graph as containment backstop).
**Exceptions/boundaries.** Island mass unreachable at any R (P255); refiner-fixpoint hazard (P303); scattered-eval lever stops paying at high d (P361 — a cost boundary, not recall); 10M pure-walk family is graph-construction-bound (SYMPACK-B, novelty-fenced).
**Untested predictions.** (1) δ predictable pre-build: fit δ ≈ g(routed coverage@p, d) across six datasets; predict R* ±1 for a held-out dataset from a 10-min pilot. (2) k* grows with n: predict k*(wiki-35M) in 64-96 with measurable containment gain from k96 — k96 never built (P360 stopped at k64). (3) Graph-off ablation matrix: benefit = f(1 − entry containment), <0.5pt on msturing at p≥96, >10pt on WebVid at any p. (4) Matched-byte entry-vs-depth trade favors seeds exactly when m_inf > A·δ^R.
**Simplifications.** Drop RobustPrune from the default IVF-seeded build (keep for cold-start walk mode); replace R/M/hops grids with the P362 pilot (estimate δ from ~20 queries, solve R); stop refining edge overlap past ~0.9 — spend on count or entry coverage; paper: one reachability equation, "benefit ~ 1 − entry containment" instead of a dimension rule.

## Lens: data-structure

### Law 1 — Edge-Budget Primacy — VERIFIED-WITH-QUALIFIERS
**Statement.** Graph recall contribution scales with materialized adjacency BUDGET (k/KEDGE, a0 copies, seeds) and — **within base-manifold kNN edge families, for in-distribution queries** — is nearly invariant to HOW edges are selected at matched budget (RobustPrune vs raw-32-nearest: +0.001-0.003, P336). **Under distribution shift with sufficient train-query density, edge COMPOSITION at matched budget is first-order**: co-retrieval vs base-kNN edges differ by whole points (P321: +1.2-1.7pt at matched 16; WebVid champion = 16 kNN + 48 co), with the co:base ratio scaling with OOD-ness (P343) and vanishing in-dist (P326) or below ~a few % query density (wiki: worth 0.0001). Adjacency is a budget WITH a direction; single-knob-k is safe only in-distribution. Quality above the ~0.65-overlap floor has geometrically diminishing (not zero) returns (P301-302: 0.874→0.965 still bought +0.3-0.45pt).
**Evidence.** P335 (k16→32 flipped DEEP); KEDGE-bracket (+5.8pt at p2, 10M); P317-318 (k8 halves the win); P336 (selection wash in-family); P295 (0.28-overlap recovers +1.0 of +1.8pt); N23/P319 (copies same currency).
**Exceptions/boundaries.** Strongest counterexample: P321 — matched-budget co-retrieval vs base edges is a ~500-1000x larger selection effect than P336's wash, in the law's own architecture mode. Cold-start walks reward diversification (GATE2); sub-floor quality is a real exception (P281-283); KEDGE at low d taxed by union-rescore cost (band win, not frontier flip).
**Untested predictions.** (1) KEDGE 96/128 on DEEP-10M: doubling gains shrink geometrically (k64→k128 < +2.5pt at p2), net QPS win only above ~0.97. (2) Currency fungibility: {a0=2,k=32} vs {a0=1,k=64} at matched bytes within ~0.5pt. (3) RobustPrune-vs-raw on wiki-35M k64: predict wash (<0.005) in seeded-cascade mode. (4) Replicate the P295 corruption sweep on wiki/WebVid: knee at overlap ~0.3-0.5.
**Simplifications.** Retire Vamana/RobustPrune build path + alpha tuning from the champion (keep as cold-start diagnostic); delete reverse-edge machinery; graph tuning = edge budget k plus (OOD only) co:base ratio; build to ~0.9 overlap and spend saved time on wider k.

### Law 2 — Materialized-Coverage Law (data-structure form) — VERIFIED-WITH-QUALIFIERS
**Statement.** Routing miss mass is an information cap of centroid summaries; not recoverable by query-time inference from the summary information the router already used (ten+ independent negatives). Scoped: (1) per-query adaptivity CAN pay when the signal is **point-level and difficulty varies** (P9 certificate, 1.9x; graph expansion itself is query-time harvesting of point-level signals). (2) "Every offline materialization succeeds" requires: (a) construction signal **independent of the capped router** (routed self-search plateaus 0.53-0.63 overlap, P292-294; router-free NN-descent reaches 0.89-0.90, P296-299); (b) query-aligned materialization needs train-query density (wiki 1.4% starves it); (c) edges harvest only repairable mass above the seed-coverage floor m_inf(p) (P311/P255). The only query-time interventions that work at cell level are O(1)-parameter calibrations (gamma).
**Evidence.** P312 (oracle 6-8x probe headroom, unharvestable); P315-316, P351, P313-314 (the negative class); P300 (+0.0pt from perfect flat routing with graph on); P205/P207 (one graph hop realizes oracle pool expansion bit-identically); P96-98 (query-trained cells lose even offline).
**Exceptions/boundaries.** Strongest counterexample (kills the "offline always succeeds" letter): WIKI-QSEED-STARVED — the law's own exemplar levers fail at 1.4% density. Tree-EM works (offline partition optimization); QSEED is offline materialization consumed at query time.
**Untested predictions.** (1) Modern learned per-query prober lands on/inside the fixed-p frontier on DEEP/cohere — closes the class. (2) Supervised multi-assignment (2nd/3rd cell by GT set-cover instead of SOAR residual) harvests more of the oracle gap than SOAR. (3) Difficulty certificates fire <15% on uniformly-hard OOD (WebVid) — adaptive deepening economically null there. (4) The P312 oracle-decomposition pilot predicts which of {p, a0, k} pays BEFORE tuning, on any new dataset.
**Simplifications.** Freeze the coverage lever space to {p, a0, k_edges, seeds, gamma}; delete adaptive-p/gbias/PRF/Wq/learned-router paths and flags; oracle-decomposition pilot becomes the standard first step on a new dataset.

### Law 3 — Manifold-Gap × Query-Density Law — VERIFIED-WITH-QUALIFIERS
**Statement.** Query-aligned structure (co-retrieval edges, QSEED, bipartite builds) is a currency whose VALUE tracks the base-vs-query manifold gap (measured as routed coverage at fixed probes) and whose AVAILABILITY requires train-query density — both factors necessary, payoff **monotone in each, not calibrated-proportional** (the density knee was never measured, and both starvation data points are in-distribution, i.e. confounded). Routed coverage predicts which CURRENCY pays, **not which architecture wins** — dimension/byte walk economics is an independent co-equal axis (DEEP loose band was closed by a NON-query-aware walk, P353, where the coverage dial predicted cascade). Query info also pays via routing-score CALIBRATION (gamma was "the mover" on t2i); only "never in partition geometry/placement" is proven (P96-98, P198, P185).
**Evidence.** P321/P322 (co-edges +1.2-1.7pt, win both axes at t2i 1M/10M); P337/QSEED-dense-WebVid (oracle-seed proved pure seed limitation; coverage 0.42→0.76 flipped the frontier); P343 (48:16 co:base at extreme OOD); WIKI-QSEED-STARVED (1.4% density → nulls; proves Roar's wiki edge is structural — degree/termination — which redirected the whole wiki play); P326 (in-dist Roar 1.6-2.4x behind).
**Exceptions/boundaries.** Strongest counterexample: DEEP-10M loose band — every factor said "cascade wins", Roar won <0.95 until genbo's own walk closed it; architecture axis is separate. Query info in cell geometry is negative even when the gap is large.
**Untested predictions.** (1) Density-threshold sweep: subsample WebVid queries 100%→10%→1.4%; gains decay to ~zero near wiki's density — numeric eligibility threshold. (2) Co-retrieval on msturing/cohere: predict ≤+0.2pt (gap-zero test). (3) 5-min routed-coverage pilot on any NEW dataset predicts the loose-band winning CURRENCY before tuning. (4) Synthetic in-dist query dilution degrades co-edge value ~linearly in induced coverage rise.
**Simplifications.** One build-time knob: co:base ratio set by measured routed coverage; auto-skip co-retrieval/QSEED builds below density/gap thresholds; retire anisotropic/query-trained partition variants permanently (three independent nulls).

### Law 4 — Self-Build Law — VERIFIED-WITH-QUALIFIERS
**Statement.** Never use **routed search as the iterated build mechanism** at high d — it inherits exactly the coverage cap the graph exists to fix (routed selfknn plateaus 0.63 overlap at d=768; iterating the capped searcher plateaus 0.53). The ban is on the loop, not seed provenance: routed output is a legitimate SEED (P297). On OOD with dense train queries, co-retrieval edges (searcher-free, not local-join) beat and complement ND — the recipe is **ND local join for base edges + query-GT projection edges when density suffices**. Warm-start dominates **only when seed quality is below the refiner's fixpoint under the build metric** (P303: i8-ND degraded a 0.99 seed to 0.9699 — build under the serving metric, one data point, treat as hypothesis). Warm-start seeding converts quality into build-budget eligibility (k64 seeded 1.29h → chain 1.46h < 2h gate, P360) — build-time eligibility is a competitive axis.
**Evidence.** P292-294 (the cap); P296-299 (ND self-build enables fully self-contained 1.23-1.93x wins vs HNSW); P314-8 (incremental patch 4.8x faster AND better than rebuild); SEEDED-NNDESCENT/P360 (gate-eligible k64 flipped the wiki high band); P279-280 (provenance audit — self-build makes wins standalone).
**Exceptions/boundaries.** Strongest counterexample (kills exclusivity): P321/P322/P343 — champion OOD graphs are hybrid, not local-join-only. Low-d where routed selfknn is near-exact (t2i 0.9917): ND unnecessary, i8 fixpoint slightly harmful. (2) Chain-seeding k16→k32→k64→k128 on wiki: each doubling ~1.1-1.6x prior step → k128 inside ~3h; 0.93-0.97 band recall rises. (3) Streaming msturing-30M: incremental ND patches hold ≥0.85 overlap for <10% of insert budget. (4) Seeding ND from a corrupted 0.28-overlap graph converges to the same fixpoint in fewer rounds (path-independence).
**Simplifications.** Delete routed-selfknn build path and routed graph-augmentation loop; provenance section collapses to one sentence (all graphs self-built, same algorithm); build-metric = one flag tied to serving metric; streaming defaults to incremental patching.

### Law 5 — Role-Reallocation Law — VERIFIED-WITH-QUALIFIERS
**Statement.** Once adjacency carries coverage, every other structure re-optimizes — mostly for cost (partition coarsens: +2pt AND +8% QPS at kf16384→4096, P325; knee ~150 pts/leaf; scale-gated — wash at 100M, P240) but sometimes for a new ROLE (the IVF scan survives as a beam seeder even when recall-dead, TINY-SCAN). The per-band architecture is set by **total scattered bytes ≈ (evals needed, set by dataset reachability δ and graph construction quality) × bytes-per-row** — P362's SYSTEM LAW — not bytes-per-hop alone (msturing d=100: cascade wins EVERY band at DEEP-like row bytes; wiki 0.93-0.955: Roar's walk wins at maximal byte toll because its construction needs far fewer evals). Routed-coverage regime (P329) is a co-equal variable. Seeds bound what hops repair **in the cascade at targets above m_inf**; the winning loose-band walk is itself 8 entries + deep narrow rounds (P356).
**Evidence.** P325/P223-224/P227 (geometry re-optimization, two-sided knee); P353/P356 (scan-bypass walk; fixed rounds beat best-first 1.06-1.17x); P361 (walk inverts at d=1024); P255/P362 (miss model, work normal form, one-dimensional tuning); P352/P354 (residual loose deficit is the fixed scan+union toll).
**Exceptions/boundaries.** P240 (no extrapolation to 100M); coarse cells without graph coverage are a pure loss (P229); P356's 0.80 corner; architecture transfer fails on byte cost alone even with sound mechanism (P361).

(3) A cheap δ pilot transfers portal-walk tuning to msturing with one free parameter. **Simplifications.** Fold architecture selection into SBANN_PRESET via the SYSTEM-LAW decision variable (not bytes alone); default pts/leaf jumps to the coarse knee with graph on; drop 3-level-router work below ~100M; tuning collapses to P362/P363 pilots.

## Lens: failure-and-adaptivity

### Byte-Cost Regime Law — VERIFIED-WITH-QUALIFIERS
**Statement.** Bytes-per-row × recall band is a first-order SCREEN for walk-vs-cascade and micro-lever triage — **not "almost entirely" decisive**. Compute bytes **per stage-tier fetch** (scan tier vs f32/fp16 rerank tier vs 4KB page granularity), not per dataset dimension: at d=96 the 384B f32-rerank rows and page scatter still make fp16 (+14.5-35.4%, P350), resident (+27.6%, P346) and graph-local physical order (+21.9%, P348) pay; only within-line byte cuts (PQ4-NAV) and row-skipping (ADAPT-STOP) are null at 2-line rows. The walk-vs-cascade rule holds **only at matched graph quality AND matched build budget** (unlimited-build Roar beats the cascade at wiki d=1024 despite a ~10x byte toll; the walk family is graph-quality-bound at 10M). Routed-coverage/distribution structure is a co-equal axis (WebVid flipped via seed coverage at exactly 512B; msturing walk loses the whole loose band at ~104B). The byte branch is a default overridable by a coverage pilot. The 10-minute ISA-width litmus (P183) still pre-classifies stages.
**Evidence.** P361 vs P353 (same walk: d=96 win 1.16-1.48x, d=1024 loss); SQ4-RUNG (+17-51% at d≥512, loses at d=96); P354 (seven streaming alternatives lose at d=96); P340-nulls (row-skipping moves QPS zero); P183/P115 (the litmus both directions).
**Exceptions/boundaries.** Strongest counterexamples: P350 (fp16 wins at d=96 — refutes "byte levers lose outright at low d") and msturing d=100 (walk loses the loose band in exactly the byte regime where walks "win"). t2i d=200 boundary is load/pressure-dependent (P345); cache-phase moves the boundary (P344); compute-bound stages are decided by arithmetic (P21/P239).
**Untested predictions.** (1) Portal walk on msturing d=100 wins loose ≤0.93 by ≥1.15x — treat as contradicted-until-measured. (2) Walk on cohere d=768 loses ≥1.5x at EVERY recall. (3) Synthetic pad sweep (DEEP rows to 128/256/512B) locates the flip between 128-256B — a hardware constant for future datasets. (4) SQ4 sidecar on cohere d=768: +15-35% at high recall.
**Simplifications.** Load-time branch on bytes/row as DEFAULT (coverage-pilot override); auto-disable SQ4/RESIDENT/F16 below threshold per-tier; kill work-skipping proposals at low d without running them; paper: one bytes/row-axis figure replaces four per-dataset explanations.

### Coverage Is Materialized, Never Inferred — VERIFIED-WITH-QUALIFIERS
**Statement.** Coverage destroyed by summarizing points into centroids is unrecoverable at query time from router-class signals — **after distribution-level global calibration (gamma-class) is applied**. Pre-gamma, a per-query controller on router features CAN sit modestly above the fixed frontier by harvesting the systematic mismatch (P205: x1.17-1.21 held-out, +10% e2e) — the correct diagnosis is "your global calibration is missing", not "adaptivity works"; post-gamma the residual is empty (P207 deferred; P313 tie; P314 saturation). Judge signals by **information content, not feature provenance**: churn is point-level information (corr 0.84-0.98) that failed on double-work COST, not learnability. Materialization is necessary, not sufficient: query-aligned forms are density-bound, and offline adaptive allocation on a geometric signal also lost (N10). Kill-rule: a proposed lever whose input is computable from features the router already scores, applied post-calibration to query-time allocation, gets zero budget.
**Evidence.** P351 (oracle 0.9817 vs learned 0.7788 < raw 0.8133 — headroom ≠ learnability, leak-free); P313-316, P96-98, N10 (≥7 independent negatives); P300 (information cap); materialization wins: P207/P321, P11/P132-133, QSEED, P296-299.
**Exceptions/boundaries.** gamma (2-2.7x probe cut) is the load-bearing carve-out and must travel with the statement; P9 (out-of-class JL-gap signal, difficulty variance required); streaming low-live-p (observable non-geometric signal).
**Untested predictions.** (1) Bandit/RL per-query probe controller on routing features lands within noise of the fixed frontier on DEEP/t2i-10M — cheap kill-test. (2) A second global scalar yields < gamma's remaining gain (class saturated). (3) An escalation gate from the int8-vs-fp16 shortlist margin at the W-th slot (P350 inverted) WILL beat fixed budgets on mixed-difficulty datasets. (4) Extra SOAR copies only for points whose offline assignment churns across EM rounds beats uniform a0 at matched storage.
**Simplifications.** Delete the learned-routing lever space (code, flags, sweeps); require a held-out predictive-power gate before any hot-path work; routing R&D reduces to partition quality + materialized adjacency budget + one-time calibration check.

### Clone-Without-Component Law — VERIFIED-WITH-QUALIFIERS
**Statement.** Copying a competitor's structural ratio or architecture without its enabling component fails (severity ~3% wash to 0.09-recall catastrophe). Ask first: "what component spends this ratio, and do we have native parity?" What transfers is **an extracted PROPERTY rebuilt on native components** — a scalar axis (edge count, probe fraction; values re-tuned per scale/regime), an ordering (P256), a numeric recipe (P113), or a full structural principle (query-aligned adjacency via native co-retrieval edges P321/P343; per-band architecture switch P353) — the fence bans cloning the construction, not re-deriving the property. The planning gate's concession branch is **provisional, not terminal**: moat attributions have a demonstrated false-positive rate (SYMPACK-B's "fenced graph is the moat" was overturned by P349's retune + P353's native closure five days later); a concession must survive per-band retuning (P349), matched-budget ablation (P336) and oracle decomposition (P337) first, and stands only until a new native mechanism class is tried.
**Evidence.** P174 (coverage-fraction clone → 0.09); P100-P106 (ScaNN-like leaves without the AH kernel, 194 vs 4084 QPS); SYMPACK-B (kernel green, recall collapse); P336 (attribution before adoption); P113/P256 (property transfers that won).
**Exceptions/boundaries.** Strongest counterexample: the SYMPACK-B→P349→P353 arc — shipping the gate's concession would have surrendered a band native mechanisms won. Kernel/numeric transfers are metric-scoped (P166-167 vs P177-178); "dead" cloned sub-mechanisms can be mode-conditional (GATE2); ratio-matching is valid as a diagnostic (P159-160).
**Untested predictions.** (1) The transferable property from Roar's wiki 0.93-0.955 edge is effective degree/termination: a native k96/k128 seeded ND graph (gate-eligible via warm-start) shrinks or closes the wiki concession without cloning. (2) ScaNN batch-mode at 10M+ without an in-register kernel ≥368 Mcand/s loses ≥3x — one-hour microbench before any build. (3) ROARMODE diagnostic on msturing: residual edge is warm-entry + termination. (4) Matched-budget ablation on the WebVid hybrid (48 co vs 48 extra base edges): co-SELECTION, not degree, carries the OOD win.
**Simplifications.** One-line planning gate on every "do what X does" proposal (name component + native parity path); clone flags are diagnostics only, out of sweeps/CI; mandatory matched-budget ablation before crediting any adopted mechanism; concessions published as "closing lever is the competitor's own construction".

### Query-Density Starvation Law — VERIFIED-WITH-QUALIFIERS
**Statement.** Query-aware materialization requires BOTH nonzero train-query density AND nonzero distribution shift; payoff is **monotone in each, not calibrated-proportional** (the density knee is unmeasured; both starvation points are in-dist-confounded). No hard coverage skip-gate: at routed coverage 0.83 (t2i, above the old ~0.8 threshold) the build still paid ~1pt mid-band plus the tail (P321/P322). Skip only when density < ~1-2%, OR shift ≈ 0, OR a P337-style oracle injection shows the seeded stage is not the binding constraint (necessary third factor: HUBSEED was dead at achieved coverage 0.62-0.70 because DEEP is not seed-limited). Inference corollary stands: Roar's wiki edge cannot be query-awareness (same starved 500k queries) — it is structural degree/termination, confirmed and redirected the wiki play (P360).
**Evidence.** WIKI-QSEED-STARVED (1.4% → coverage 0.034-0.065, co-edges 0.0001); QSEED-dense-WebVid (0.42→0.761, frontier flip); P337 (oracle-seed aimed the build); P343 (co-edge budget scales with OOD-ness); P326 (in-dist worthless at any density).
**Exceptions/boundaries.** Strongest counterexample: t2i co-retrieval win ABOVE the law's old skip threshold — refutes the threshold form, not the two-factor structure. Extreme scale gates builds before density does (t2i-100M Roar 10.5h/degenerate); density never rescues query-trained partitions (P96-98).
**Untested predictions.** (1) Subsample WebVid queries to 1.4%: coverage collapses <0.15, flip erased — clean causal test. (2) Co-edge sweep at 10%/1%/0.1% of t2i queries: knee between 1-3%. (3) Synthetic query augmentation does NOT recover coverage (density ≠ information). (4) msturing co-edges ≤0.1pt at any density.
**Simplifications.** 10-minute pre-build gate = density + routed coverage + binding-stage check (replaces per-dataset build debates); delete wiki hybrid co-edge artifacts (worth 0.0001); co:base ratio becomes a formula of measured shift.

### Band-Level Adaptivity Law — VERIFIED-WITH-QUALIFIERS
**Statement.** Every large (≥1.2-1.3x) win over "fixed budgets" came from OFFLINE re-scaling per band/scale/containment (P111 2x; P154-158 1.98x; P349 1.84x→1.29x; P215; P363), never from per-query online adaptivity. Sharpened: **per-query adaptivity never beat a properly provisioned/calibrated fixed budget without an out-of-class signal** — P205's ridge adaptive-p did post +10% e2e, but only because fixed p54 was over-provisioned; gamma captured the same waste for free, after which adaptivity tied the frontier. Read a measured adaptive win as a DIAGNOSTIC that the fixed budget is off-plateau. Within-query, fixed synchronous select-truncate rounds beat the adaptive priority-queue walk 1.06-1.17x at short horizons (P356) — regularity beats adaptivity when bookkeeping can't amortize. Genuine per-query wins require (a) measured difficulty variance, (b) an out-of-class gating signal, (c) an amortizing horizon (P9; streaming low-live-p; SBANN_ADAPT_RERANK's banked tail-only help, P154). The honest form: banded policy ladder with jointly-scaled stages (P347/P362), H=E+(R−1)B universal, δ from a pilot.
**Evidence.** P349 (2216-point retune: most of the "architectural" gap was excess fixed effort); P313-314, N10, ADAPT-STOP-POOL (the null family); P356 (fixed rounds win 0.83-0.90, lose only 0.80 corner 0.990x); P9 (fires 8% on uniformly-hard data); P362 (zero-error work accounting).
**Exceptions/boundaries.** The graph walk is per-query-adaptive WORK but structural (follows materialized edges) — and improves when made fixed-round; streaming live-set size is an exact out-of-class signal.
**Untested predictions.** (1) Per-query escalation gate on the fp16 shortlist margin saves 10-30% at zero recall cost on bimodal-difficulty datasets, ~0% on unimodal — test the variance histogram first (1 hour). (2) Gating round R+1 on measured round-R improvement beats fixed R at the ≥0.98 tail only. (3) Two-tier cheap-pass/escalate-uncertified beats the balanced preset exactly where the difficulty histogram is bimodal. (4) P349-style per-band retune on WebVid and msturing shrinks remaining "architectural" gaps another 1.2-1.4x.
**Simplifications.** Delete dead per-query adaptive machinery (adapt-stop, patience, churn gates, margin multi-assign); per-dataset tuning = two pilots (δ + containment); retire the "adaptive X" idea class unless variance evidence + out-of-class signal are named upfront.

## Lens: methodology

### Same-Window Interleaving Law — VERIFIED-WITH-QUALIFIERS
**Statement.** On a shared box, cross-window QPS comparisons are invalid: same-config drift reached 3-4x (P244, P288, P331). A throughput comparison is valid **when** measured paired (ABBA/pairwise alternation) in one load window, with warm-up matched and variance sources (warm-up state, cache phase, storage backend) matched or disclosed — the full ceremony (least-busy-core pinning, SMT accounting, per-record fault/load/ctx stamping) is required only to resolve small effects (<~10-15%; unpaired estimates inflated ~3-5x there); medium effects (≥~1.2x) were reliably established with plain interleaving or quiet serial brackets. The claimable-margin bar is **residual variance under the paired protocol** — NOT historical window-to-window variance: pairing cancels shared drift (P346-abba banked +2.04%; P358 settled P355's frozen 1.03-1.16x join band at 1.269x by tracing the baseline's "window swing" to warm-up asymmetry). The variance bar applies to UNPAIRED or protocol-asymmetric comparisons and unexplained arm-intrinsic variance.
**Evidence.** P66 (±30% load noise, opposite-sign delta); P110/P137-138 (claimed 1.2-1.4x win reversed to 1.5-2x loss same-window); P187/P190 ("22x behind" collapsed to 2.22x); P211 (Optuna "winners" were load artifacts); P141-143 (load sensitivity is architecture-specific, biasing unpaired comparisons directionally).
**Exceptions/boundaries.** Deterministic metrics (recall, pool size, coverage) survive drift; a pristine box permits serial best-of-N brackets; bimodal cache-phase variance (WEBVID-BIMODAL) is not cured by pairing — the best-of/median convention must be disclosed.
**Untested predictions.** (1) Re-running sampled cross-window "wash" verdicts under ABBA flips ≥20% of those with nominal 1.1-1.5x effects. (2) Same-config QPS is bimodal on memory-pressured mmap configs, unimodal on resident — never tested systematically. (3) A load-vs-QPS regression over the ledger retro-predicts historical window "winners" to ~10%.
**Simplifications.** The permanent ABBA harness (P346-abba) replaces all ad-hoc best-of-5 scripts; delete cross-window normalization factors from analysis code (invalid per P344); one protocol section in the paper replaces per-result load caveats.

### Opponent-at-its-Best Law — VERIFIED-WITH-QUALIFIERS
**Statement.** A head-to-head claim requires: baseline at its official or self-swept best config, through its intended fast-path API, at thread/memory parity, its bugs fixed, warm-up/repetition/quoting conventions matched, AND your own side re-tuned under the current protocol (stale own-configs understated genbo ~2x, P339). These conditions are necessary but **not sufficient** — add: (a) **referee verified** (GT excludes queries, dtype/precision-correct, id+distance checked — referee bugs flipped verdicts by MORE than any config violation); (b) **margin exceeds measured environmental variance** (P355 parity-pending demotions). Violation magnitude: **~1.5x to unbounded** (P358 warm-up swing 0.85x→1.27x; MSTURING-roar-bug zero-recall). Budget-ineligibility must be established by searching the opponent's parameter space for a compliant point (P342/BUDGET-ROAR: "no compliant configuration exists" is the fair verdict) — applied to memory budgets too, not just time.
**Evidence.** P76 (single-threaded ScaNN read 4x low); P247 (sed-hack vs batched-parallel API = 8x); P217-219 (leaf-count audit swung 2.5x); P134-135 (thread parity 2x); P136 (parity fixes cut both ways — honest gap got LARGER); P339 ("never cite a baseline you haven't run yourself").
**Exceptions/boundaries.** Strongest counterexample (to sufficiency): P271-273 wiki GT contamination — every opponent-config condition satisfiable, verdict still entirely false; flip magnitude exceeded the law's whole 2-8x evidence range. Hard resource ineligibility is a reportable result (P334 ScaNN 270GB; P257; P341-t2i100M); clone-modes are diagnostics only (NOVELTY-FENCE).
**Untested predictions.** (1) Any never-reproduced third-party number moves >1.3x when re-run locally (both directions). (2) Re-tuning recorded configs untouched since P335 (t2i-100M, msspacev) improves the recorded genbo frontier ≥1.3x. (3) Retroactive matched-warm-up shifts pre-P358 ScaNN ratios 10-30% without changing winners at >1.5x margins.
**Simplifications.** One versioned baseline runbook per competitor (config, parallel API, warm-up, d%8 pre-pad); abolish quoting leaderboard/paper numbers (deletes hardware-normalization fudge factors); two-row eligible/unlimited scorecard replaces disqualification prose.

### Referee-First Law — VERIFIED-WITH-QUALIFIERS
**Statement.** Validate the benchmark before the algorithm: GT generated end-to-end by one trusted native pipeline, official precision (int8-domain GT silently caps at 0.9543, P153-157), real data (N9), query rows excluded from base (P271-272: 38% of wiki GT ids were query rows → 0.62 phantom ceiling, weeks lost), dtypes matched (P330: both systems scored ~0), verified on ids AND distances (P327), regenerated per subsample (N14), harness eligibility constraints read from source (P161). Bug signatures: recall FALLING as budget rises is unconditional (P132, P30); budget-knob INVARIANCE is a bug/pipeline signature **only well below the expected or independently-known ceiling or where the knob should bind** — at saturation, exact invariance is normal knob-not-binding physics and often a banked speedup (P363: W16 == W256, exploited as a 16x width cut), or an upstream knob binding (P168-169). The invariants detect broken pipelines generally, not the referee specifically.
**Evidence.** P271-272/P259-270 (the wiki incident: fix moved 0.62 → 0.9125-0.9896 after a multi-week ladder proved the pipeline innocent); P330; P327; P153-157; P161/P151-152 (the harness metric determined the whole strategy).
**Exceptions/boundaries.** Strongest counterexample (scopes the invariance clause): P363's genuine-physics 16x-nested invariance. When GT is the object of study, a labeled internal GT is legitimate; if the referee audits clean, the elimination ladder remains the fallback.
**Untested predictions.** (1) An automated preflight GT audit (query-in-base overlap, dtype equality, id/distance cross-check, GT-self-recall==1.0, subsample freshness) run on WebVid/DEEP GT files finds at least one latent issue. (2) Any future dataset where ALL methods land below ~0.7 at generous budgets traces to a referee defect. (3) Recall-vs-budget monotonicity assertions in the bench driver mechanically catch the next P132-class bug.
**Simplifications.** One shared native `make gt` pipeline (f32, queries-excluded, id+dist verified, per-subsample) deletes every hand-rolled GT script; preflight referee-audit script replaces multi-week elimination ladders as the FIRST response to a plateau; eligibility constants parsed from the official runner.

### Drift-Immune Metric Law — VERIFIED-WITH-QUALIFIERS
**Statement.** When environmental noise exceeds the effect size, steer by load-independent observables: recall at matched settings, pool size at matched recall, coverage fractions, survivor counts, bit-identical checks. Scoped: (1) drift-immune means immune to **CPU-load** drift only, and only **after the referee is validated** (the wiki phantom wall was steered entirely by deterministic recall — deterministic and wrong; load-immunity ≠ validity). (2) Profile FRACTIONS are not immune to memory-side contention (page-cache thrash shifts per-row cost 10-100x and phase shares qualitatively, P341/P242/P344) and are config-specific. (3) The "~1.5x QPS noise" bound is a floor: same-config swings of 1.6-3.8x are documented; the operative rule is P355's — a margin counts only if it exceeds the measured variance of that environment. Decompose every gap into recall-reach (trustworthy) vs QPS (confounded); before multi-week investment, design the cheapest decisive experiment expressible in drift-immune metrics (P176/P184; P144-145's 20-min bake-off killed a week-long project). Oracle decompositions and containment gates are this law applied to steering.
