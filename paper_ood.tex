\title{Routing Calibration for Out-of-Distribution Vector Search:\\
Why $L_2$ Partitioners Misroute, and a One-Parameter Fix}

\begin{abstract}
Approximate nearest-neighbor (ANN) search at scale is dominated by inverted-file
(IVF) methods that route a query to a small set of partition cells and then scan
quantized codes within them. Partition cells are learned on the \emph{database}
distribution, typically by $L_2$ $k$-means. We show that this is systematically
\emph{miscalibrated} when queries are drawn from a different distribution than the
base --- the out-of-distribution (OOD) regime exercised by cross-modal retrieval
(e.g.\ text queries against image embeddings under inner product). The routing
score decomposes as $\gamma\lVert c\rVert^2 - 2\,q\!\cdot\!c$ with $\gamma=1$ for
$L_2$; we show the $\lVert c\rVert^2$ term over-penalizes exactly the high-norm
centroids that inner-product-relevant neighbors concentrate near, and that a single
global recalibration $\gamma\!<\!1$ removes most of the loss at zero query-time cost.
We compose this with a $k$-NN-graph coverage step and exact float re-ranking, and
report a corollary that reverses standard practice: once graph coverage is present,
\emph{coarser} IVF cells outperform fine ones, because routing cost, not coverage,
becomes the binding constraint. On the big-ANN text2image benchmark, evaluated under
a same-hardware, tight-pairwise protocol against ScaNN at its official configuration,
the resulting engine attains higher QPS at matched recall@10 across 1M, 10M
\pending{and 100M} scales, and applies to the streaming track as well.
We treat the evaluation methodology --- which repeatedly caught measurement mirages
during development --- as a first-class contribution.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}
% --- the OOD hook (NeurIPS-shaped): distribution shift, not "our system is fast" ---
Vector search underlies retrieval-augmented generation, cross-modal search, and
recommendation. The dominant scalable family is IVF+quantization: partition the
database into $C$ cells, at query time \emph{route} to the $p\!\ll\!C$ most promising
cells, \emph{scan} compressed codes inside them, and \emph{re-rank} a shortlist
exactly. Routing quality --- does the true neighbor's cell get probed --- caps
end-to-end recall, and the partition is learned on the database.

We study the regime where the query distribution differs from the database
distribution. This is not a corner case: the big-ANN ``OOD'' track (text2image-10M)
pairs image-embedding \emph{base} vectors with text-embedding \emph{queries} under
inner product, and it is exactly where the strongest published systems separate.
Our starting observation is that an $L_2$-trained partitioner, which is optimal for
in-distribution queries, is \emph{miscalibrated} out of distribution in a precise,
correctable way.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{A diagnosis (\S\ref{sec:miscal}).} The IVF routing score is
    $s(q,c)=\gamma\lVert c\rVert^2-2\,q\!\cdot\!c$ with $\gamma=1$ recovering $L_2$
    and $\gamma=0$ recovering pure inner product. Under OOD query shift the
    $\gamma=1$ norm penalty misranks the cells that hold inner-product neighbors.
  \item \textbf{A one-parameter fix (\S\ref{sec:gamma}).} A single global $\gamma$,
    fit once, reaches the same recall at roughly half the probes, at zero
    added query-time cost (it is a per-cell additive bias).
  \item \textbf{Graph coverage and a coarse-cell corollary (\S\ref{sec:graph}).} A
    $k$-NN-graph union over the shortlist restores the coverage fine cells provide;
    given it, \emph{coarser} cells win, because they make routing cheaper without
    losing recall. We bracket the resulting geometry knee empirically.
  \item \textbf{A rigorous same-hardware evaluation (\S\ref{sec:method},
    \S\ref{sec:eval}).} We give a measurement protocol robust to shared-machine
    load, and results at 1M/10M\pending{/100M} plus streaming, with per-lever
    ablations. We disclose the protocol's limits (single machine; ScaNN as the
    measured baseline, leaderboard gaps to binary-only systems as inference).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Evaluation Methodology}
\label{sec:method}
% This section is deliberately detailed: reproducible ANN comparison is itself a
% contribution, and the discipline below caught several mirages during development.
We report \emph{same-hardware ratios}, never cross-machine QPS. All comparisons run
on one physical machine; each competitor is measured in the same session, seconds
apart, at its own best operating point.

\paragraph{Protocol.} For a target recall (here recall@10 $\ge 0.90$), we (i) fix
each system's operating point by an independent sweep, requiring both to clear the
recall gate against the \emph{same} exact ground truth; (ii) alternate the two
systems round to round (order flips each round) so neither owns a load window;
(iii) take best-of-$k$ within a round and report the \emph{median of per-round
ratios}; (iv) recompute recall from raw result ids against a float ground truth,
never trusting a cached number. On a shared machine, load drift between two builds
minutes apart exceeds the effects being measured, so only \emph{interleaved}
same-window ratios and load-independent recall are trusted; absolute QPS is not.

\paragraph{Warm-resident semantics.} Both systems are measured warm and resident,
matching leaderboard serving. A subtle asymmetry --- one system resident across
rounds while the other re-spawns and re-faults its index --- biased an early run;
we score each engine's warm block.

\paragraph{Pristine confirmation.} For headline numbers we pause all other
first-party jobs (SIGSTOP) so the measurement window is genuinely quiet, and confirm
across $\ge 20$ rounds.

\paragraph{The baseline at its best.} ScaNN is configured from its \emph{official}
big-ANN recipe (e.g.\ 40{,}000 leaves for text2image-10M, anisotropic AH with LUT16,
residual quantization, bfloat16 re-ordering) and given its own leaves/re-order sweep,
including corner configurations. A win that depends on a handicapped baseline is
quarantined; we describe one such quarantine (an under-leaved cached index) and its
correction.

\paragraph{What we do not claim.} These are same-hardware ratios on one (shared)
machine, not a submission on the standardized challenge VM; and ScaNN is the measured
opponent --- gaps to binary-only systems (e.g.\ the leaderboard's top entry) are
stated as \emph{inference} from the public leaderboard, not head-to-head.
