## Deletion-order notes

- The RESID family (SBANN_RESID, SBANN_RESID_DPB, SBANN_RESID_ITERS, SBANN_RESID_REFINE, SBANN_RR_DEPTH, SBANN_RRLIST) deletes as one unit — the sub-knobs are dead-with-parent.
- SBANN_VNNI and SBANN_VNNI_AB delete as a pair (the A/B flag is meaningless without the toggle).
- SBANN_USE512FS delete must NOT touch the distinct default-ON SBANN_USE512/USE512I16 (P241) kernel.
- SBANN_PQ4_NAV + SBANN_PQ4_FILE (both UNSURE) would join the DELETE list if the paper line-1404 negative claim is accepted as prose-only provenance (or shown to have run through an offline gate script rather than this engine flag) — resolve before the deletion pass.
- SBANN_BEAMS (UNSURE) should be decided jointly with SBANN_LEVELS (KEEP-champion): deleting BEAMS strands SBANN_LEVELS-based deep (L>=4) hierkn builds for 1B scale.
- SBANN_RESIDQ (UNSURE) is independent of the RESID family (different mechanism: residual-frame primary codes vs 8-bit refine sidecar) and does not block that deletion.
- SBANN_FASTSCAN (KEEP-diagnostic) is a prerequisite of DELETE-candidate SBANN_USE512FS; if USE512FS is deleted and the sec:neg wider-SIMD claim re-anchored, SBANN_FASTSCAN's keep rationale weakens to the FASTSCAN2 A/B only.
