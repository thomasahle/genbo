If a robust mixture has total opportunity occupation at most `H0`, then `tau=1/H0` is automatically below the water level, so the defect is bounded by the raw scaled moment `sum ell^alpha ((H0 a/ell^alpha)^p - 1)_+ / p`. For polylogarithmic route depth and `p = Theta(1/log log n)`, the depth normalization is constant; the next geometric target no longer has to solve for the water level.

The exact remaining defect can be bounded by `sum ell^alpha log_+(H0 a/ell^alpha)`, so a very concrete sufficient target is bounded charge density `H0 a <= C ell^alpha` on occupied unstable opportunities and total unstable occupation `H0 = O(log log n)`. The positive-moment target remains useful when bounded density fails on a small tail.
